## Summary

The highest-priority blockers are missing programmatic form labels in `ApplyForm`, `ArticleForm`, `SettingsForm`, and `JobsSearch`; inaccessible modal semantics/focus management in `ApplicationModal`; and absent or weak focus indicators across public navigation, cards, links, and form controls. Several dynamic states also need live-region support, especially form errors, loading spinners, search empty states, auto-save feedback, and modal success messages.

## Recommended Remediation Order

1. Fix form labels, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`, and announced errors across public and admin forms.
2. Implement accessible modal dialog behavior: `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal`, focus trap, Escape close, initial/return focus.
3. Add global focus-visible styles and component-level focus states that meet WCAG 2.2 Focus Appearance.
4. Add skip links and mobile navigation parity for the public header.
5. Hide decorative icons/SVGs from assistive technology and provide accessible names for icon-only controls.
6. Add live regions for loading, success, error, search result, and status update feedback.
7. Audit color tokens and opacity-based text for WCAG AA contrast, especially white/60 on teal and subtle focus rings.
8. Add reduced-motion handling for smooth scroll, spinners, card transforms, image scaling, and animated pulse effects.

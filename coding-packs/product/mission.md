# Product Mission

## Problem
HR teams need a professional recruitment website and admin CMS to publish jobs, manage applicants, and keep recruitment news updated without developer involvement.

## Users
- HR/admin users manage jobs, news, applications, and settings through CMS screens.
- Candidates browse jobs, read company/recruitment content, and apply through public pages.

## Differentiation
Design-first execution: the product should preserve the polished Professional Tech Hub design direction from `.design` while turning it into a production-ready Next.js + Supabase SaaS-style application.

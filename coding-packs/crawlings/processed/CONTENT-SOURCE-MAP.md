This document tracks the origin of all data points in the processed mock data package.

| Data Point | Category | Source Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Company Name/Slogan | Settings | `crawled_raw_data.json` |

## Disclosure of Synthetic Content
- All job postings (id: job-1 through job-6) are synthetic mock data designed to demonstrate a typical Fabbi recruitment pipeline. They do not represent active real-world listings and were generated to match the technical domains and service offerings identified during the crawl.

## Conflict Resolution
- Where crawl data lacked Japanese translation for specific leadership titles, the translation was generated by machine and flagged with `translationStatus: "machine_draft_needs_review"`.
